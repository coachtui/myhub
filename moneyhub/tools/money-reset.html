The ten-minute Money Reset

Four monthly estimates in, one clear starting point out. Rough numbers are better than waiting for perfect ones.

Your four numbers

Use last month, or a typical month. Sort spending by how you feel about it, not by category: required, worth it, not worth it. Nothing here is a judgement.

Your numbers never leave this page. They are not saved, sent, or tracked.

What you will get

One number: what is left after required costs. Then what that means for you this month, and one next move. Blank fields count as zero, and estimates are fine.
