CNN’s Fear & Greed Index reads 31.9 (“Fear”), essentially flat versus the previous close of 31.4. A week ago the index was at 25.1 (deeper fear); a month ago it was 53.0 (“Neutral”); a year ago it was 77.6 (“Extreme Greed”).

Price near highs with sentiment in Fear is historically a contrarian-bullish setup — positioning hasn’t caught up to price, which leaves room to run if data cooperates. But it also means a single bad headline (a Hormuz escalation, a hot PMI/CPI print) lands on an already-nervous tape and can move price disproportionately.
