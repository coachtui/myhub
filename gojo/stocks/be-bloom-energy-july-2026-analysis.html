The Business (Not the Stock)

Bloom Energy makes solid oxide fuel cell systems — the Bloom Energy Server — that convert natural gas, biogas, or hydrogen into electricity on-site. It's an electrochemical reaction, not combustion, which means lower emissions and higher efficiency than a gas turbine. Customers get reliable, always-on power that bypasses grid constraints.

That last point is the AI hook. Data centers need uninterruptible, dense power. The grid can't build fast enough. Bloom's proposition — put a fuel cell behind your data center fence, skip the utility queue — is exactly the message hyperscalers want to hear. Schwab Network called BE a "reliable energy aspect" of the AI infrastructure trade as recently as July 22.
