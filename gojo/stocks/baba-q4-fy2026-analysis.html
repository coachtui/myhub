Alibaba (BABA) Q4 FY2026 — AI Cloud at 40%, Value Valuation, Messy Earnings

Cloud +40%, AI revenue growing triple-digits for 11 straight quarters, and a new $2.5B dividend. FCF turned negative and profit cratered. Cheap if the investment pays off — the market seems to think it will.

The Earnings (May 13, 2026)

Alibaba reported Q4 FY2026 with a mixed headline but a stock that went up anyway — shares surged ~6-7% after the print. That tells you what the market actually cares about right now.

  • Q4 Revenue: RMB 243.38B (~$35B) — missed estimates (consensus: RMB 247.09B)
  • Full-year revenue: RMB 1.023T (~$148B), up 3% YoY (11% ex-disposed businesses)
  • Cloud Intelligence Group revenue: +38–40% YoY
  • AI-related cloud revenue: triple-digit growth for the 11th consecutive quarter
  • AI now accounts for ~30% of external cloud revenues
  • Non-GAAP net profit: down ~62% YoY — heavy investment spending
  • Free cash flow: turned negative (from +RMB 73.8B to -RMB 46.6B)
  • New $2.5B annual dividend announced

The market looked past the revenue miss and profit collapse because the AI cloud trajectory is accelerating and management is making a clear bet: spend now, harvest later.

The Cloud + AI Story

This is the whole thesis, and the data keeps printing. Cloud Intelligence Group external revenue grew 40% YoY in Q4. That’s not a one-quarter fluke — 11 straight quarters of triple-digit AI revenue growth is a trend. CEO Eddie Wu projected AI revenue could exceed 50% of cloud segment revenue within the next 12 months.

Alibaba’s Qwen model series is at the center of this. They’re not just reselling OpenAI — they’ve built their own frontier model stack and are deploying it across their cloud infrastructure. China doesn’t run on AWS. Chinese enterprises run on Alibaba Cloud, and AI is making that stickier.

The playbook is identical to what AWS did for Amazon a decade ago: use the installed enterprise base to monetize an AI platform layer. BABA is ~10 years behind AWS in cloud maturity but running the same playbook in the world’s second largest economy.

The Valuation Case

This is where BABA stands out. You’re not paying a growth multiple for this AI story.

  • Current price: ~$130–$146 range (has been volatile post-earnings)
  • Trailing P/E: ~21x
  • Forward P/E: ~19x
  • 10-year median P/E: ~27.76x — current is 24% below that
  • Short interest: ~1.94% — very low, not a squeeze setup
  • Buybacks: $12.5B+ spent in FY2024; ongoing program reducing share count
  • New $2.5B annual dividend (first meaningful dividend in company history)

For comparison: ZS trades at 27x forward on 16-17% ARR growth. BABA trades at 19x forward with 40% cloud growth. The discount exists because of China risk — but the gap is wide.

Why the Profit Collapsed

The non-GAAP profit decline looks alarming on paper. It’s not a business deterioration story — it’s a deliberate capex cycle. Alibaba is spending aggressively on:

  • AI cloud infrastructure build-out (data centers, GPU clusters, Qwen R&D)
  • Quick commerce (Taobao Instant Delivery) — competing with Meituan for same-day delivery dominance
  • International expansion (AliExpress, Lazada, Trendyol)

FCF turning negative is the clearest signal of how hard they’re stepping on the gas. This is the same pattern AMZN ran in 2014–2016 before AWS margins flowed through. The bet is that cloud + AI infrastructure spending today generates durable margin expansion by FY2027–2028.

If that happens, 19x forward earnings looks very cheap in hindsight. If it doesn’t, the multiple re-rates lower on margin pressure and you have a compounding problem.

The China Risk (Always)

You can’t analyze BABA without this. The risk stack:

  • Regulatory: Beijing can (and has) disrupted Alibaba’s business model overnight. The Ant Financial crackdown in 2020 cost the stock 70%+ from its highs. That risk doesn’t go away.
  • Geopolitical: US-China tensions, tariffs, potential ADR delisting risk. The stock trades in New York as an ADR — if relations deteriorate sharply, access to US capital markets is not guaranteed.
  • Domestic competition: Pinduoduo (PDD), JD.com, and Douyin (TikTok’s China side) are all taking e-commerce share. BABA’s core Taobao/Tmall business is under structural pressure.
  • Jack Ma factor: He returned publicly in 2023, but the company operates under a different political reality than it did pre-2020.

The China discount is real and has been earned. The question is whether it’s 30% too wide or appropriately sized.

The Setup

BABA is the rare case where you’re getting a legitimate AI cloud growth story at a value multiple. That only happens when there’s a risk premium attached — and China is that premium.

If you believe:

  • The AI cloud investment pays off in 2–3 years
  • US-China relations don’t deteriorate into ADR delisting territory
  • Beijing stays constructive on tech after years of crackdowns

…then 19x forward on 40% cloud growth is deeply mispriced. The $2.5B dividend and ongoing buybacks show management thinks so too.

If you’re skeptical of any of those three conditions, the discount is deserved and the FCF deterioration is a warning sign, not a feature.
