Zscaler (ZS) Q3 FY2026 — Beat, Guidance Up, AI Security Tax Is Real

Revenue +25.4%, ARR +25%, FCF positive, and every enterprise AI deployment is a new customer event.

The Setup

ZS reported Q3 FY2026 on May 26 — two days ago. This is fresh data, not a stale thesis.

  • Revenue: $850.5M (+25.4% YoY) — beat ($835.6M estimate)
  • EPS: $1.08 vs. $1.00 estimate — beat by $0.08
  • ARR: +25% YoY
  • Free Cash Flow: $136M (positive and growing)
  • Stock reaction: +1.2% — calm, not euphoric

Q4 FY2026 guidance: $1.08–$1.09 EPS (above estimates). Full-year FY2026 guidance: $4.10–$4.11 EPS (above estimates).

Why ZS Fits the AI Efficiency Conversation

AMZN and NOW are AI absorption plays — companies that use AI to compress their cost structure and expand margins. ZS is the inverse: it’s the security tax on the efficiency wave.

Every company deploying AI — every agent, every cloud workload, every API connection — creates a new attack surface. Zero trust security isn’t optional when you’re running AI at enterprise scale. ZS processes nearly 1 trillion AI/ML transactions annually across ~9,000 organizations. They’re already embedded in the infrastructure before most companies even know they need them.

The AI efficiency wave doesn’t happen without the security layer. ZS is that layer.

The Business

ZS is the market leader in Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA). Instead of a VPN/perimeter approach, everything is verified and proxied through Zscaler’s cloud. Why this model wins in an AI world:

  • AI agents need their own security policies — a browser can’t enforce identity; ZS can
  • Shadow AI (employees using unauthorized AI tools) is a real enterprise risk ZS can monitor and control
  • Cloud-to-cloud traffic — the new attack surface — is exactly what ZS is built to inspect

ZS published a 2026 AI Threat Report: 83% YoY surge in AI activity across enterprise networks. That’s their TAM growing in real time.

The Numbers

  • TTM Revenue: $3.17B (+24.6% YoY)
  • Q3 Revenue: $850.5M (+25.4% YoY)
  • Forward EPS FY2026: ~$4.10–4.11
  • Current price: ~$185
  • Forward P/E: ~45x
  • Price/Sales: ~9x TTM
  • GAAP Net Margin: -2.25% (negative, improving)
  • FCF: $136M this quarter (positive and growing)

The Q2 Context

Earlier this year ZS dropped 16% after Q2 earnings despite beating revenue. The market punished a billings or RPO metric concern. That selloff created the setup — Q3 just confirmed the fundamentals are intact and guidance is moving up, not down.

The stock recovering calmly (+1.2%) on Q3 vs. a violent selloff on Q2 tells you the overreaction has mostly corrected.

The Honest Take

ZS is not cheap at 45x forward earnings. That’s the primary pushback. But for a company growing 25% on a $3B+ base with improving FCF and a product that becomes more necessary as AI scales — it’s a fair price for the quality.

The bull case doesn’t require AI to accelerate ZS’s growth. It just requires AI to keep expanding the attack surface, which it will by definition. Every new AI deployment is a new ZS customer event waiting to happen.

Competition is real — Palo Alto (PANW) and CrowdStrike (CRWD) are both fighting for the same enterprise security wallet. But ZS has the clearest positioning on zero trust / AI traffic inspection. Platform consolidation in enterprise security tends to favor the leader.

The Risks

  • Enterprise security spending gets cut in a macro slowdown
  • PANW or CRWD undercuts on price to win consolidation deals
  • GAAP profitability still negative — any multiple compression hurts
  • A billings miss (like Q2) can cause outsized selloffs regardless of revenue

The Bottom Line

ZS is the security infrastructure for the AI efficiency wave. AMZN and NOW make money when AI makes them more efficient. ZS makes money because AI makes the world less secure. Different thesis, same macro tailwind.

If you’re building a portfolio around AI transformation, ZS is the defensive position within that theme — the company that wins whether AI deployment goes smoothly or not.

At ~$185, post-Q3 beat, with guidance moving up and the Q2 overreaction mostly digested — this is a cleaner entry than it was six months ago.
