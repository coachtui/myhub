Macro Snapshot

The macro backdrop is unchanged from last week’s data, with one important update. Unemployment is 4.2% after a September payroll gain of only 29,000; August PCE ran 3.4% headline and 3.0% core year over year; and Q2 GDP was revised up to 2.2%. The Fed raised rates 25bp to 3.75%–4.00% on September 16 (its first hike since 2023), and the minutes released October 7 showed broad support for the hike and most participants expecting one more by year-end — but no signal that it comes in October. Market-implied odds of an October 27–28 hike are roughly 20–25%.

The pressure point is rates and energy. Gasoline averaged $4.355 per gallon in September, 7.3% above August, which is why Wednesday’s CPI is expected to show headline inflation re-accelerating.
