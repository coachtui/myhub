The Business

MYR Group (NASDAQ: MYRG) is an electrical construction contractor. Two segments: Transmission & Distribution (T&D) — high-voltage transmission lines, substations, and distribution work for utilities — and Commercial & Industrial (C&I) — electrical work on data centers, transportation, healthcare, and industrial facilities. In plain terms: they build and upgrade the grid and the electrical guts of large buildings. That puts them directly in the path of three multi-year tailwinds — grid hardening and replacement, electrification, and the data-center power buildout.

This is a labor-and-execution business, not a product business. Gross margins run ~12%, net margins ~4%. Contractors like MYRG don't win on pricing power; they win on bidding discipline, project execution, and not blowing up fixed-price jobs. The financial quality here is real: ROE 24.7%, ROIC 25.6%, net cash on the balance sheet, and a Piotroski F-Score of 8. That's a well-run contractor, which is rarer than it sounds.

  • Cyclicality: Utility and commercial capex budgets can tighten. Backlog is visibility, not a guarantee — projects can slip or cancel.
